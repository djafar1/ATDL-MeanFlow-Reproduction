MeanFlow learns average velocity over a time interval for one-step generation~\cite{meanflow}. Its training target uses a Jacobian-vector product. It trains from scratch without a pretrained teacher or distillation.

We target Table~1(a), the ratio of sampling $r\ne t$; Table~1(b), Jacobian-vector-product computation; and Table~1(e), loss metrics. The completed loss-metric study uses the authors' JAX code on ImageNet-1K at $256\times256$. The other experiments' datasets, implementations and results await integration. Fr\'echet Inception Distance (FID) assesses generation quality; we disclose departures from the released setup.
