\textbf{Metric.} FID-50K is the paper's main quality metric and our sole measure. It combines fidelity and coverage, making failure diagnosis difficult; precision/recall and human assessment would provide complementary evidence~\cite{generative-precision-recall}.

\textbf{Models.} Table~2 includes Shortcut, IMM, DiT and SiT~\cite{meanflow}, but no standalone Rectified Flow baseline. Rankings depend on sampling and training budgets; they are not controlled comparisons at matched training compute. Our B/4 ablation cannot verify the larger-model claims.

\textbf{Datasets.} Generation is evaluated on ImageNet-256 and CIFAR-10~\cite{meanflow}, leaving other domains and background/noise corruptions untested. VTAB-1k's 19 tasks illustrate broader coverage but measure classification transfer rather than generation~\cite{vtab}. Generalization beyond the reported benchmarks remains unverified.
